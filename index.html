About Our Studio

Your Trusted Dental Care Partner

Welcome to SmileCare Dental Studio, where advanced dental science meets compassionate patient comfort in the heart of Chennai.

We believe a visit to the dentist should be reassuring, gentle, and transparent. Our clinic is equipped with ergonomic treatment operatories, low-radiation digital imaging, and strict hospital-grade sterilization protocols. Whether you require a routine preventive cleaning or complex cosmetic smile restoration, our approach is always tailored to your personal oral health goals.

Preventive

General Dentistry

Teeth Cleaning & Polishing

Gentle ultrasonic scaling to remove stubborn tartar, plaque buildup, and coffee or tea stains, followed by prophy enamel polish.

  • Freshens Breath & Protects Gums
  • Painless Ultrasonic Technology
Aesthetic

Teeth Whitening

In-office laser whitening designed to brighten enamel by up to 3 to 6 shades safely in a single session with minimal sensitivity.

  • Immediate Noticeable Results
  • Enamel-Safe Formulation
Restorative

Root Canal Treatment

Save infected or damaged natural teeth with painless rotary endodontic therapy, finished with a precision-fit dental crown.

  • Single-Sitting Available
  • Pain Relief & Tooth Preservation
Surgical

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-feeling titanium and zirconia implants to replace missing teeth, restore full chewing ability, and prevent bone loss.

  • Lifetime Durability
  • Looks & Feels Like Natural Teeth

Braces & Aligners

  • Discreet & Removable Aligners
  • Custom Digital 3D Simulation

Cosmetic Dentistry

  • Digital Smile Design (DSD)

Pediatric Dentistry

Caring, tear-free dental visits for toddlers and teens. Fluoride treatments, dental pit & fissure sealants, and early cavity interception.

  • Cavity Prevention Treatments
